# Booker Evidence Programme (R2)

The only source of real buyer evidence for B2's rubric. It cannot wait: rubric v1 is due on Day 12.

**Sample:** 12 interviews, three groups of four: in-house corporate event managers, agency/production planners, bureau agents. UK-based; booked ≥ 3 paid speakers in the past year. Recruit through Propela's bureau partners first, then direct LinkedIn outreach. £50 donation or voucher.

**Guide (30 min)**
1. Walk me through the last three speakers you booked. Why them?
2. Tell me about one you shortlisted and dropped. What tipped it?
3. What in a session description makes you shortlist? What makes you skip?
4. What do you check about a speaker before you put them in front of a client?
5. Card sort: rank the six v0 criteria plus Franzoni's four traits (relevant, known by the right people, memorable, easy to work with). Add or remove any.
6. Rate three sample drafts (one strong, one generic, one over-claimed) and say why.

**Option:** run interviews through A1's interview engine with a booker script (tests A1 on a second use; transcripts arrive timestamped). Keep a human-led option for bookers who decline an AI interviewer.

**Coding:** a criterion enters v1 only if ≥ 4 of 12 raised it unprompted or ranked it top three. Wording comes from interview quotes, stored in the Canon with the interview id.

**Acceptance:** ≥ 8 interviews by Day 10 (≥ 2 per group); rubric v1 signed off; calibration targets met by Day 18 (`evals/calibration.ts`), or launch with "provisional" kept on.
