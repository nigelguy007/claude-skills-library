# AI value strategy

Skills authored in this repo for working out what the AI economy means for one specific business.

- **ai-value-strategist**: a staged review (intake, challenge, research, value-map triage, threat model, scored options, experiments, AI operating model, positioning, QA) that ends in a decision memo with tagged evidence and owned next steps. It uses the public [Signals at Scale value map](https://www.signalsatscale.com/valuemap.html) as its lens.

It was built by reverse-engineering an AI-written strategy handoff. The handoff was strong at challenging the owner's framing, mapping a framework onto the business and writing a clean narrative. It was weak on evidence, numbers, option comparison, threat timing and ownership of next steps. This skill keeps the first set of qualities and enforces fixes for the second through intake, evidence tags, a fixed scoring rubric and a blocking QA gate.

It deliberately does **not** produce a "transformation platform" of ten linked documents. The output is one memo plus four short registers, because a small business needs a decision and three experiments, not a programme office.
