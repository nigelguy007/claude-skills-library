# Value map: 7 functions, 14 zones, 70 categories

Source: Signals at Scale, *Value in the emerging AI-mediated information ecosystem*, v1.0, September 2026. Interactive map: https://www.signalsatscale.com/valuemap.html · Report: https://www.signalsatscale.com/reports.html

The categories are provisional and the authors expect the map to change. Treat each zone as a lens for asking questions, not as a forecast.

**Capture labels** (who the report expects to keep the value):
- **CONCENTRATED**: a few players capture it, usually whoever holds the user interface, the transaction or the liability. A small business can win here only by being the trusted party, not by building the platform.
- **CONTESTED**: open, and decided by who owns exclusive access, context or trust.
- **NOBODY**: society benefits but nobody can easily charge for it. Rarely a revenue play for a single business; sometimes a reputation play.

## Questions for triaging a single business
Ask these questions per zone and score relevance from 0 to 3:
1. Does the business do work in this zone today, even if it doesn't call it that?
2. Does it hold an asset here that a customer's AI agent can't reach: private history, judgement, exclusive access, trust?
3. If the capture label is CONCENTRATED, who is the likely concentrator, and is the business it, a supplier to it, or squeezed by it?
4. What would the business lose if this zone were fully automated by someone else within 3 years?

Intermediaries (agencies, brokers, marketplaces, advisers) usually come out highest on Z06, Z08–Z10 and Z14. Z09 (decision delegation) is where the customer's own AI agent threatens the intermediary's margin; Z14 (embodied and social) is often their defensive moat.
