# QA checklist (Stage 9)

Run every check against the draft. Fix failures, then report the counts in memo section 9. Don't hand off with a failing **blocking** check.

## Blocking
- [ ] **No invented numbers.** Every figure is client-stated, cited, or a formula with labelled inputs. Search the draft for £, $, %, "k", "m" and digits, and trace each one.
- [ ] **Every material claim is tagged** (`[Evidence]`, `[Client-stated]`, `[Inference]`, `[Hypothesis — test: …]`).
- [ ] **The owner's first hypothesis was explicitly challenged** and a verdict was recorded.
- [ ] **At least 3 options were scored on the same criteria**, including status quo plus and the owner's original idea.
- [ ] **Every experiment has a pass threshold, an owner and a date.**
- [ ] **No agent or automation is proposed without a named data source and a human approval gate.**
- [ ] **Every open question sits in the decision register with an owner and a date.**

## Consistency
- [ ] The recommendation in the header matches section 5.
- [ ] Each high-likelihood threat (T#) is addressed by the recommended option or an experiment, or the memo says why not.
- [ ] Each experiment (E#) tests a hypothesis that the recommendation depends on.
- [ ] Positioning lines don't contradict the category the strategy keeps (e.g. a line that sounds like a consultancy when the strategy says "stay a speaker agency").
- [ ] Section 3 drops at least 9 of the 14 zones, or justifies keeping more.

## Calibration
- [ ] Confidence in the header matches the share of `[Hypothesis]`/UNKNOWN inputs behind the recommendation.
- [ ] No sentence says "will" about the future without an `[Inference]` tag.
- [ ] Praise for the business's strengths is backed by an example (a past case, a client quote) or tagged `[Client-stated]`.

## Length
- [ ] The memo body is no more than about 2,500 words. Anything longer goes into an appendix or gets cut.
