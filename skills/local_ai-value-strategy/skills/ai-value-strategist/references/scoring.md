# Option scoring

Use the same five criteria for every option. Score 1–5, then show the table **and** the one-line reason behind each score. Criteria weights are defaults; change them only if the owner states different priorities, and record the change.

| Criterion | Weight | 5 means | 1 means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Protects current revenue | 25% | No risk to existing income or discoverability | Puts the core line at risk |
| Value at stake | 25% | Large upside from a driver formula with sourced inputs | Small, or not measurable |
| Defensibility vs. customer-side AI | 20% | Rests on an asset an agent can't reach (private history, trust, judgement, embodied access) | Rests on public information or admin work |
| Confidence | 15% | Inputs are `[Evidence]` or `[Client-stated]` | Inputs are mostly `[Hypothesis]` or UNKNOWN |
| Effort / time to first proof | 15% | Testable in under 30 days at low cost | Needs a build or hire before any learning |

**Weighted score** = Σ(score × weight). Report it to one decimal place, and never present it as precise. The point is to make trade-offs visible, not to compute an answer.

## Value-at-stake driver formula
Write each new revenue idea as a formula with labelled inputs, for example:

```
Advisory retainers = (# existing clients likely to buy [Hypothesis — test: E1])
                   × (price per year [UNKNOWN — needed: pricing test])
                   × (conversion from offer [UNKNOWN])
```

If two or more inputs are UNKNOWN, score Value at stake as "unscored" and Confidence as 1. That's the honest result, and it tells the owner which experiment to run first.

## Required options
1. **Status quo plus**: keep the model, add the cheapest defensive moves.
2. **The owner's original hypothesis**, even if Stage 1 challenged it, so the rejection is on record.
3. **The revised direction** from Stage 1.
4. Optionally, one or two **genuinely different** alternatives (a partnership, a niche focus, productising an asset).

## Kill criterion (per option)
"We stop pursuing this if **<observable result>** by **<date>**." Example: "fewer than 2 of 8 interviewed clients say they'd pay separately for advisory by 31 January."
