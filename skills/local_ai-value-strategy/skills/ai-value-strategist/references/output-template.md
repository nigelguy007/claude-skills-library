# Output template

One decision memo (~2,500 words max) plus appendices. IDs are shared across all sections: H = hypothesis, T = threat, O = option, E = experiment, D = decision, R = data request.

---

## <Business> — AI value strategy · <date> · v<n>

**Decision needed:** one sentence.
**Recommendation:** one sentence naming the option (O#) and the first experiment (E#).
**Confidence:** Low / Medium / High, with the single biggest UNKNOWN that holds it down.

### 1. Central question and the challenge
- Central question.
- The owner's starting hypothesis (H1), quoted.
- The strongest counter-argument, with evidence tags.
- Verdict: keep / revise / reject, and the revised hypothesis (H2).

### 2. What the evidence says
Competitors (named, with URLs), buyer search language, signs of customer-side AI adoption. Every line tagged.

### 3. Where value moves for this business
The 3–5 zones from triage, each with: asset owned, capture label and what it implies, risk. Then one line listing the zones dropped and why.

### 4. Threats
The threat table (T1…), with likelihood, timing, exposed revenue line and leading indicator.

### 5. Options compared
Scoring table (O1…), with reasons and a kill criterion per option. Recommendation, and why each other option lost.

### 6. What to test in the next 90 days
Experiment table (E1…), with pass threshold, owner and decide-by date.

### 7. AI operating model (if earned)
Data-readiness verdict first. Then the sequenced build, with an approval gate and metric per agent. Or: "Fix knowledge capture first: <specific steps>."

### 8. Positioning (if in scope)
Category vs. proposition. Candidate lines scored on the three questions, the sameness check against competitor lines, the discoverability check, and the A/B test to run.

### 9. QA report
Untagged claims: 0. Invented numbers: 0. Contradictions found/resolved: n. Open UNKNOWNs: list the R#s.

### Appendix B — Data request
| R# | Data needed | Decision it unlocks | Likely source | Owner | By |

### Appendix C — Experiment plan
| E# | Tests hypothesis | Method | Cost/effort | Pass threshold | Owner | Decide by |

### Appendix D — Decision register
| D# | Decision / question | Status (decided / open / parked) | Options considered | Rationale | Owner | Due | Depends on (E#/R#) |

Open questions are only allowed in the decision register, and each one needs an owner and a date.
