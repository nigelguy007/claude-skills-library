# Fundraise Run

Turn a broad raise into a staged, reviewable process with a smaller qualified target list and an evidence trail.

## Establish the brief

Read [references/input-contract.md](references/input-contract.md). Continue with available inputs, label unknowns, and ask only for gaps that would materially change target selection. Treat the requested amount, instrument, valuation, and deadline as founder inputs—not validated recommendations.

## Coordinate the pack

Use the sibling skills as needed:

1. Position: `$round-definition`, `$fundraising-narrative`, `$evidence-room-audit`, `$investor-fit-thesis`.
2. Map: `$fund-universe-builder`, `$fund-mandate-verifier`, `$cheque-fit-analyzer`, `$fund-behavior-profiler`, `$portfolio-conflict-check`, `$partner-fit-mapper`, `$warm-path-mapper`.
3. Engage: `$outreach-sequencer`, `$intro-request-writer`, `$vc-outreach-writer`, `$investor-meeting-brief`, `$partner-question-builder`.
4. Run: `$fundraising-crm-review`, `$term-sheet-comparator`, `$diligence-room-manager`, `$fundraising-close-plan`.

Read [references/evidence-standard.md](references/evidence-standard.md) before any investor research. A fund remains unqualified until stage, cheque, geography, sector, portfolio conflict, activity, and partner ownership have been checked or marked unknown.

## Deliver the control sheet

Return:

- round brief and narrative;
- qualified investor table with source URLs, access dates, fit reasons, conflicts, partner, warm path, confidence, and next check;
- sequenced outreach waves and meeting briefs;
- diligence readiness and open issues;
- live pipeline with owner, last touch, next action, due date, probability as founder judgment, and blocker;
- close plan with dependencies and decisions requiring counsel or board approval.

Keep sourced facts, inferences, and founder assertions visibly separate. Never promise investment, invent a warm introduction, infer private contact data, or describe an investor as committed without direct evidence.
