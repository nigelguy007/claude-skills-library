# Fundraise input contract

Collect what is available:

- company, product, website, headquarters, operating markets, sector, and business model;
- round type, amount sought, currency, instrument, target close, minimum close, and intended use of funds;
- current revenue or usage metrics, growth window, retention, burn, runway, cap table summary, and prior financing;
- defensible proof points, known weaknesses, regulatory constraints, and material customer concentration;
- investor exclusions, geography constraints, strategic-investor policy, and competitive sensitivities;
- existing target list, prior conversations, committed or soft-circled capital, and relationship map;
- data-room inventory, legal counsel, board approval requirements, and founder availability.

Mark every item `provided`, `verified`, `inferred`, or `unknown`. Do not delay initial research for non-critical unknowns. Do not upload confidential documents to an external service or share them beyond the user's authorized scope.
