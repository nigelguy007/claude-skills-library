# Investor research evidence standard

## Source order

Prefer evidence in this order:

1. Official fund mandate, team, portfolio, news, fund-close, and investment announcements.
2. Portfolio-company financing announcements and regulatory filings.
3. Government or regulator records such as SEC IAPD/Form ADV, EDGAR, Companies House, or the relevant national register.
4. Reputable databases and reporting as discovery or corroboration, with their date and limitations.
5. Professional social posts only for recent statements attributable to the named person; never use a profile snippet as sole proof of a current role when an official source is available.

## Record every claim

For stage, cheque, sector, geography, recent activity, partner ownership, or conflict retain: exact claim; source URL; publisher; source date when shown; access date; quoted or paraphrased support; evidence type; confidence; and limitation.

Separate:

- `sourced fact`: directly supported;
- `inference`: reasoned from facts, with explanation;
- `unknown`: not established publicly;
- `founder input`: supplied by the user and not independently verified.

Use exact dates. “Recent” requires a stated review window. Do not convert fund size into cheque size without evidence. A regulator record confirms disclosed identity or filing information, not current investment appetite. A portfolio overlap is a potential conflict to investigate, not proof the fund will decline.

Use public professional information only. Do not infer emails, phone numbers, personal addresses, signing authority, or willingness to meet. Recheck volatile facts immediately before outreach.
