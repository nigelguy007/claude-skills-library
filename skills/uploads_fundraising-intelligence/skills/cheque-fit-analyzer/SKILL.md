# Cheque Fit Analyzer

Compare the company's requested investor allocation with explicit cheque ranges, dated financing participation, disclosed lead behavior, ownership targets, and the specific active vehicle.

Use an evidence band rather than false precision: `documented range`, `observed examples`, `inferred plausible`, or `unknown`. Explain every inference. Exclude total round size when the fund's contribution is undisclosed; a fund appearing in a syndicate does not reveal its cheque. Do not convert assets under management or vehicle size into a first-cheque estimate without an evidenced policy.

Deliver fit as strong, plausible, weak, or unknown, plus the evidence, contradictions, and one question that would resolve uncertainty. Include whether the proposed allocation is likely material enough for the fund and compatible with founder ownership constraints, but label both as hypotheses unless directly supported.
