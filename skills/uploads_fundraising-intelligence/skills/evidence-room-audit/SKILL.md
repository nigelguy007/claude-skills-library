# Evidence Room Audit

Inventory documents by category: corporate, cap table, financing history, financials, commercial metrics, customers, product and IP, security and privacy, people, tax, regulatory, contracts, and board approvals.

For each item record owner, version date, source system, confidentiality level, access status, and whether it supports a claim used in the pitch. Cross-check recurring facts—revenue, cash, burn, runway, customer count, headcount, ownership, and round terms—across the deck, model, cap table, board materials, and narrative. Report discrepancies; never choose the most favorable number.

Deliver `ready`, `stale`, `missing`, `inconsistent`, `restricted`, and `not applicable` lanes, plus a prioritized remediation queue. Separate pre-meeting materials from diligence-only and counsel-only items. Recommend least-privilege access and a disclosure log. Never expose personal identifiers, credentials, customer secrets, or privileged legal advice in a public artifact.
