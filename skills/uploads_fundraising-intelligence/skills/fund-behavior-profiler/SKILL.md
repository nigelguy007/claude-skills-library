# Fund Behavior Profiler

Define a review window, normally the latest 24 months, and collect dated investments from official fund and portfolio-company announcements. Record company, date, announced round stage and size, fund participation, named partner when supported, geography, category, and source.

Separate new investments from follow-ons, accelerators, scout checks, strategic programs, and investments made by a different vehicle. Do not infer the fund's cheque from total round size. Compare observed activity with the current website thesis and label alignment, drift, sparse evidence, or contradiction.

Deliver the most relevant recent examples, activity cadence, stage and theme patterns, and what remains unknown. A quiet public record may reflect privacy rather than inactivity. Do not equate announcement count with investment quality, available capital, or likelihood of investing.
