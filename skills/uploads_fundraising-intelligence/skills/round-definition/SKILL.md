# Round Definition

Build a one-page round brief from founder inputs and current operating evidence.

Capture the amount sought, minimum viable close, currency, instrument, timing, current runway, use of funds, milestone unlocked, prior capital, ownership constraints, target investor types, geography, strategic-investor policy, and explicit exclusions. Reconcile the amount with the stated plan; flag arithmetic or timing gaps instead of silently repairing them.

Deliver `provided facts`, `verified evidence`, `open decisions`, and `assumptions` separately. Show a base plan and, when evidence supports it, a smaller minimum-close scenario. Treat valuation, dilution, tax, securities rules, and instrument choice as decisions for qualified legal and financial advisers.

End with an investor-screening contract: stage, plausible cheque range, ownership tolerance, sector, geography, lead/follow preference, required capabilities, and conflict policy. Do not start fund ranking until this contract is reviewable.
