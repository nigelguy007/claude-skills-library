# Intro Request Writer

Require a verified connector-to-investor path, the connector's relationship context, the investor fit reason, and the company's current round narrative. If the path is speculative, say so and draft a permission check rather than an introduction request.

Create two parts: a personal note to the connector and a short forwardable blurb. The note should explain why this investor, why the connector is being asked, and make declining easy. The blurb should include the company, problem, specific proof, round, and one evidence-backed reason the investor fits.

Do not claim the connector endorses the company, inflate familiarity, reveal confidential metrics, or pressure them with invented urgency. Deliver subject line, note, forwardable blurb, facts requiring founder verification, and the next follow-up date. Draft only; sending requires explicit authorization.
