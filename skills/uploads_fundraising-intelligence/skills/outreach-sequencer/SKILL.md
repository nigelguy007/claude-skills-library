# Outreach Sequencer

Build waves from the qualified list, not the original universe. Consider fit, partner match, warm-path readiness, conflict risk, evidence freshness, expected learning value, decision speed, lead/follow role, geography, founder capacity, and target-close timing.

Use early meetings to test the story without placing the highest-priority firms first. Keep enough density to create momentum without overwhelming follow-up or diligence. Explain every sequencing decision; do not invent investor interest or use a false deadline.

Deliver waves with target date, fund, partner, route, objective, prerequisite, message variant, owner, and fallback. Include a learning loop after each wave: objections heard, narrative changes, evidence requests, and whether the next wave should proceed. Any status such as interested, partner meeting, diligence, or soft circle must come from a recorded interaction.
