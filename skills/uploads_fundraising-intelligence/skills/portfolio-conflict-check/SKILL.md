# Portfolio Conflict Check

Start from the company's product, customer, business model, data advantage, geography, and near-term roadmap. Search the fund's official current and exited portfolio, then verify relevant companies through their own current websites and recent announcements.

Classify each overlap as `direct`, `adjacent`, `shared customer`, `shared channel`, `strategic complement`, or `unclear`. Record the compared facts, source URLs, dates, and why the overlap matters. Distinguish a fund-level portfolio overlap from a partner's active board or investment relationship.

Deliver a risk tier, disclosure recommendation, safe first-meeting boundary, and questions for the fund. Never state that the fund is legally conflicted or will decline. Do not disclose confidential roadmap or customer information merely to improve the screen. Escalate legal, fiduciary, or NDA questions to counsel.
