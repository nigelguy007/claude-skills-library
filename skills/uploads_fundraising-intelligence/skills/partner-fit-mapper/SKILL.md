# Partner Fit Mapper

Use official team biographies, board roles, authored investment announcements, portfolio attribution, and dated professional statements. Verify that the person is current and invests from the relevant office or vehicle.

For each candidate record role, evidenced themes, relevant deals, board conflicts, geography, recency, and source. Separate `deal owner`, `category evidence`, `seniority only`, and `unknown`. A partner title does not prove the person owns a sector; a social profile does not prove current signing authority.

Deliver one primary partner, one fallback when supported, the fit rationale, contradictions, and an opening hypothesis tailored to their actual work. If no partner is evidenced, say so and recommend the firm's official intake path or a question to resolve ownership. Use public professional information only; never guess email patterns.
