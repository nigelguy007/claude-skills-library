# Fund Universe Builder

Use the investor-fit thesis as the inclusion contract. Discover funds through official portfolio and team pages, financing and fund-close announcements, regulator records, founder disclosures, and reputable databases used as leads.

Normalize firm names, management entities, funds, brands, and offices without collapsing distinct vehicles. Retain discovery source, official website, operating geography, claimed stage/sector, and the next verification needed. Deduplicate by firm and specific vehicle while preserving aliases.

Deliver a longlist with `discovered`, `eligible for verification`, `rejected`, and `unclear` lanes. Every rejection needs a reason. Do not rank a fund until mandate, cheque, recent behavior, portfolio conflicts, and partner ownership have been checked. Counts describe the researched universe, not the entire market, and inclusion never implies that a fund is deploying or accepting pitches.
