# Fundraising CRM Review

Work from an authorized export or user-provided table. Normalize fund, partner, stage, owner, last interaction, next action, due date, source notes, and commitment status. Preserve original values and report ambiguous merges.

Check for overdue actions, records with no owner, stage claims unsupported by notes, inactive targets, duplicate funds, one-partner dependency, wave imbalance, diligence bottlenecks, and target-close risk. Silence is not rejection or interest. Probability is a founder judgment unless an explicit model and evidence are supplied.

Deliver a weekly control sheet: decisions needed, overdue follow-ups, meetings to prepare, evidence requests, risks, and next seven-day actions. Separate recorded fact, inference, and proposed status change. Any live CRM mutation, external follow-up, or reassignment requires explicit authorization.
