# Investor Fit Thesis

Translate the round into testable fit dimensions: investor type, stage, first-cheque range, fund vintage and deployment status, sector thesis, geography, lead/follow behavior, ownership targets, reserve strategy, board expectations, relevant operating support, and portfolio conflict tolerance.

Set `must have`, `useful`, `neutral`, and `disqualifier` criteria. Give each criterion an observable test and an acceptable evidence source. Do not weight criteria until the founder states priorities; if missing, produce an unweighted screen.

Deliver a reusable qualification rubric and three examples: strong fit, plausible fit with an unknown, and reject. A large fund, famous partner, shared keyword, or portfolio logo is not fit by itself. Do not derive cheque size mechanically from assets under management or fund size. State which criteria are facts about the company, founder preferences, legal constraints, or hypotheses to test.
