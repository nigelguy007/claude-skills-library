# Fundraising Narrative

Build an answer-first story: why this company, why now, why this market, why this team, what has been proven, what remains risky, and what the round unlocks.

Start from supplied metrics and source documents. For every quantitative claim preserve the metric definition, period, cohort or denominator, source, and date. Label projections and founder beliefs; do not blend them with historical results. Challenge claims that depend on an undefined market, vanity metrics, cherry-picked cohorts, or a causal story the evidence does not establish.

Deliver:

- a 25-word company line;
- a two-minute spoken narrative;
- an eight-part investor storyline;
- proof points and the evidence behind each;
- the three hardest objections and honest responses;
- claims to remove or substantiate before use.

Keep language specific and falsifiable. The goal is coherence under questioning, not exaggerated certainty.
