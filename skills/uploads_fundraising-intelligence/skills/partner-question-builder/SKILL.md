# Partner Question Builder

Turn research gaps and contradictions into a short, prioritized question set. Cover only what matters for the current meeting: thesis fit, cheque and ownership, active vehicle, partner sponsorship, decision path, follow-on reserves, relevant portfolio overlap, operating support, timing, references, and the next internal milestone.

For each question state the unknown it resolves and how the answer changes the founder's decision or process. Prefer open, neutral wording. Avoid questions already answered clearly in current public sources, performative diligence, requests for confidential fund information, or language that assumes commitment.

Deliver must-ask, useful-if-time, and later-stage questions, plus warning signs and a factual post-meeting capture template. The skill prepares questions; it does not score character, predict behavior, or substitute for legal diligence.
