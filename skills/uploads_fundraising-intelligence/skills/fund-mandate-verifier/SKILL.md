# Fund Mandate Verifier

Resolve the investing entity and active vehicle first. Check the official investment thesis, current team, portfolio, fund-close announcements, portfolio-company financing releases, and regulator filings where applicable.

For each mandate dimension report `supported`, `contradicted`, or `unknown`, with URL, source date, access date, and limitation. Distinguish what the firm says from what its dated investments show. Use a stated review window for activity and note when evidence belongs to an older fund, departed partner, different office, or adjacent strategy.

Deliver a short verdict: eligible, provisional, or reject. Explain contradictions such as a “seed” label paired with recent Series A investments rather than forcing one answer. Regulatory records can corroborate identity, affiliated entities, and disclosures; they do not prove current appetite. Recheck volatile facts before outreach.
