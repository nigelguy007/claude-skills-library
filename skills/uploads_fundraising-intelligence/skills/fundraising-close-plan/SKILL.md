# Fundraising Close Plan

Map the path from current status to minimum and target close. Capture investor, amount, status supported by evidence, conditions, decision maker, lead/follow role, diligence requests, document dependencies, board and shareholder approvals, counsel owner, signature sequence, funds-flow requirements, and date.

Separate committed, signed, soft-circled, interested, and unconfirmed capital. Never upgrade status from optimistic language or a meeting alone. Build a critical path and identify dependencies such as lead terms, allocation, legal documents, regulatory checks, option-pool decisions, consents, and banking verification.

Deliver minimum-close and target-close views, owners, dates, blockers, contingency paths, and communications drafts. Mark legal, tax, accounting, governance, and funds-flow decisions for qualified advisers. The plan must not send, sign, accept, announce, or transfer funds without explicit authorization.
