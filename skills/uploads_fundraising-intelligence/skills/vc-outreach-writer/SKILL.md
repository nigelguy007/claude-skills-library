# VC Outreach Writer

Start from the verified partner brief. Use one real reason for the match—relevant investment, thesis, operating focus, or portfolio pattern—and connect it directly to the company's round. Do not cite generic biography phrases as personalization.

Draft a compact message containing company, concrete proof, why now, round context, why this partner, and a low-friction request. Provide email and professional-network versions only for channels the user plans to use. Keep sensitive metrics out unless approved.

Deliver the copy, subject lines, sources behind the personalization, claims the founder must verify, and one follow-up. Reject false familiarity, invented referrals, unverified portfolio facts, guessing email addresses, or saying a fund is likely to invest. Sending or enrolling messages in a sequence requires separate explicit authorization.
