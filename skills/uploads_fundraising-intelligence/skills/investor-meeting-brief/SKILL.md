# Investor Meeting Brief

Combine the current company narrative with the fund's verified mandate, recent behavior, relevant portfolio, partner evidence, cheque fit, conflict screen, relationship history, and meeting stage.

Deliver a one-page brief: meeting objective, participants and roles, three fit hypotheses, likely questions, evidence-ready answers, founder questions, sensitive topics, contradictions, desired next step, and facts to confirm live. Attribute investor facts with URLs and access dates.

Tailor the brief to the stage: first call tests fit; partner meeting tests conviction and ownership; diligence resolves evidence; term discussion clarifies economics and governance. Do not invent internal fund process or partner opinions. Flag confidential items that should not be volunteered and legal or financial questions that require counsel.
