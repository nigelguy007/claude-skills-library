# Term Sheet Comparator

Extract each actual term without filling gaps: security or instrument, valuation, investment amount, option-pool treatment, liquidation preference, participation, conversion, anti-dilution, dividends, board, voting and protective provisions, pro rata, information rights, founder vesting, exclusivity, conditions, fees, and closing timeline.

Normalize definitions before comparing. Show raw language, plain-language interpretation, company impact, investor impact, interaction with other terms, and open question. Use the current NVCA model documents as a reference point when applicable, not as a claim that a term is standard or acceptable.

Deliver a matrix, scenario-sensitive economic summary, control and execution risks, negotiation priorities supplied by the founder, and a counsel question list. Never recommend signing, provide a legal conclusion, or infer missing terms. Mark superseded drafts and protect confidential documents.
