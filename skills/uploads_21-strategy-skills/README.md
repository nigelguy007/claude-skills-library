# 21 Consulting-Style Strategy Skills for Claude

A downloadable pack of 21 standalone Claude skills for strategy work, grouped into six
engagement domains: diagnose, map markets, choose strategy, build execution, govern value,
and communicate. Each skill runs a named method and works on its own or as part of a full engagement.

## What's inside

### Diagnosis & Framing
1. **Situation Assessment** (situation-assessment) - Triangulates financial, market, and operational data into a fact-based baseline, separating confirmed fact from inference and open unknowns.
2. **Growth Barriers** (growth-barriers) - Decomposes stalled growth through the funnel and cohort data to isolate the single binding constraint, not the loudest complaint.
3. **Assumption Audit** (assumption-audit) - Surfaces the unstated beliefs a strategy rests on, grades the evidence behind them, and stress-tests the ones that would break the plan.

### Market & Competitive Intelligence
4. **Market Mapping** (market-mapping) - Triangulates top-down and bottom-up sizing and maps demand against supply quality to find attractive, under-served white space.
5. **Competitive Intel** (competitive-intel) - Models rival capabilities and incentives to predict their most likely next moves and pre-commits your response.
6. **Customer Segmentation** (customer-segmentation) - Builds needs-based, mutually exclusive segments scored on attractiveness and right-to-win, not generic personas.
7. **Profit Pool Analysis** (profit-pool-analysis) - Maps revenue and margin by value-chain stage to reveal where profit actually concentrates and where it's migrating.

### Strategic Choice & Economics
8. **Strategic Options** (strategic-options) - Generates a genuine option set across time horizons and reasons to a conditional recommendation instead of advocating one answer.
9. **Pricing Strategy** (pricing-strategy) - Diagnoses whether pricing is a value, structure, or discipline problem and produces a sequenced pricing action plan.
10. **Business Case Builder** (business-case-builder) - Builds a driver-based economic model with NPV/IRR, sensitivities, and the conditions required for the case to hold.
11. **Portfolio Review** (portfolio-review) - Reallocates capital and attention across a portfolio using evidence on attractiveness, strength, and actual returns, not inertia.

### Operating Model & Execution
12. **Operating Model Design** (operating-model-design) - Turns a strategy into the capabilities, structure, and decision rights needed to actually deliver it.
13. **Initiative Prioritizer** (initiative-prioritizer) - Cuts a long initiative list to the few the organization can actually execute, sequenced by dependency and capacity.
14. **Transformation Roadmap** (transformation-roadmap) - Sequences a prioritized plan into phased execution with named owners and a concrete first 90 days.

### Risk, Performance & Value Governance
15. **War Gaming** (war-gaming) - Runs a structured, multi-role simulation against realistic competitor and stakeholder reactions to surface failure modes before launch.
16. **Risk & Mitigation** (risk-mitigation) - Builds a risk register scoped to the specific strategy, with owners, mitigations, and leading indicators, not a generic list.
17. **KPI Architect** (kpi-architect) - Designs a metrics system linking one north-star outcome to operational KPIs, with action thresholds and gaming checks.
18. **Value Realization** (value-realization) - Tracks every promised benefit through a value ledger and gates funding on value actually being proven.

### Alignment & Executive Communication
19. **Stakeholder Alignment** (stakeholder-alignment) - Maps who must say yes by influence and interest and designs the pre-wiring plan to get them there.
20. **Narrative Builder** (narrative-builder) - Structures the recommendation around one governing thought and evidenced arguments so it lands fast and survives scrutiny.
21. **Decision Memo** (decision-memo) - Converts finished analysis into a one-page, answer-first memo naming the exact approval being requested.

## Install
1. Unzip this file.
2. Add the skill folders to a Claude Project as Project Knowledge, or copy them into `~/.claude/skills/`.
3. In a conversation, name the skill you want (for example, "Use the market-mapping skill"). Claude runs it with the framework already loaded.

Each skill is one folder with a single `SKILL.md`. Keep the whole set or pull out just the folders you need.
