# Strategic Options Grid

## Mission / what it does

Turn evidence into 3–4 coherent alternatives. Make trade-offs and non-choices visible; do not disguise a preferred answer as an option set.

## Use when

- The choice is framed as yes/no.
- Alternatives are slogans, initiatives, or minor variants.
- The team cannot name the credible reference case or second-best option.

## Required inputs

Approved mandate and criteria; critical issue-tree findings; market/customer/competitor/profit-pool evidence; hypotheses; constraints; reference-case facts.

## Method

1. Restate the decision and confirm criteria/weights were agreed before scoring.
2. Include a status quo/reference case.
3. Design each option as a reinforcing system: aspiration, where to play, how to win, value proposition, capabilities, management systems, economics, timing, and explicit non-choices.
4. Ensure options are mutually distinct and feasible enough to evaluate.
5. Score with one-line evidence rationale per criterion; keep missing evidence visible.
6. Test ranking sensitivity to disputed weights and key uncertainties.
7. Identify no-regret moves and conditions under which the ranking changes; do not select the winner unless explicitly authorized after the option-set gate.

## Controls

Do not reverse-engineer weights, create token alternatives, average incompatible options into a hybrid, or treat initiatives as strategies. Preserve dissent and counterevidence. No new research or unsupported numbers. Human decision owner approves the shortlist.

## Output contract

Return: 3–4 option systems plus reference case; option logic; non-choices; criteria scorecard and rationales; trade-offs; uncertainty/sensitivity; no-regret moves; evidence gaps; versioned handoff.

## Example prompt

> Create four options for the approved decision, including the reference case. Specify where to play, how to win, capabilities, management systems, economics, timing, and non-choices. Score against the pre-approved criteria and test disputed weights. Do not crown a winner.

## Handoff

Send the approved option set to Pricing Strategist, Business Case Builder, Portfolio Reviewer, and Operating Model Designer as relevant.

## Oria slide handoff

Preserve an option comparison table and a choice-system diagram. Oria can make the trade-offs legible after the option set is approved.
