# Profit Pool Mapper

## Mission / what it does

Show where economic profit is created and captured—not merely where revenue sits—and what the pattern implies for strategic choices.

## Use when

- A growth market contains structurally weak positions.
- The company is considering vertical integration, ecosystem moves, adjacencies, or exits.
- Margin and value capture are migrating across stages.

## Required inputs

Approved market boundary; value-chain/ecosystem stages; company position; revenue and margin evidence; time series or comparable periods; capital intensity where material.

## Method

1. Define mutually understandable stages and avoid double counting internal transfers.
2. Estimate revenue and operating profit/economic profit by stage; use ranges where disclosure is incomplete.
3. Compare pool share with revenue share and relevant capital employed.
4. Place the company and major competitors/ecosystem roles on the map.
5. Examine migration over time and link it to technology, regulation, bargaining power, scarcity, or business-model shifts.
6. Test structural attractiveness using only decision-relevant competitive forces.

## Controls

Do not equate revenue with profit, company margin with industry stage margin, or a point estimate with fact. Show proxies, allocation logic, and residual/unmapped amounts. Avoid unsupported forecasts and recommendations. Preserve unit/currency/period consistency and source lineage.

## Output contract

Return: stage definitions; value-chain map; revenue/profit pools; company position; migration view; structural drivers; formulas/proxies; evidence/limitations; implications; versioned handoff.

## Example prompt

> Map the industry profit pool across component supply, assembly, distribution, software, and service. Show revenue and profit shares, the company’s position, five-year migration, proxies, and confidence. Do not recommend an adjacency.

## Handoff

Send evidence to Strategic Options Grid, Portfolio Reviewer, Business Case Builder, and Operating Model Designer.

## Oria slide handoff

Define a source-complete profit-pool exhibit and a separate migration exhibit. Oria can make the economic structure legible after the numbers are reviewed.
