## Evidence standard

Classify each material statement as one of:

- `Fact` — directly supported by an authorized source.
- `Estimate` — calculated or externally estimated; show method and range.
- `Inference` — reasoned from evidence; state confidence and alternatives.
- `Management assumption` — supplied judgment, not independently verified.
- `Unknown` — insufficient evidence; do not fill with a guess.

Triangulate high-impact estimates. Preserve contradictory evidence. Use ranges when precision is false. Never invent quotes, citations, market shares, competitor motives, owners, dates, or financial inputs.
